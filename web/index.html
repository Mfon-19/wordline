Try your own footage

A fixed-camera clip works best: camera traps, bird feeders, wildlife cameras. Up to 60 seconds and 50 MB.

Your clip is processed on our server, visible only through its own link, and deleted after 24 hours. People are never tracked, and anything that looks like a person is pixelated.

How to read this

  • Each block is one video clip. Time runs left to right; the picture is at each end.
  • The sharp sheet is the current moment. Drag the bar at the bottom, or press ← →, to move it.
  • Each coloured tube is one animal's visit, found and traced by OpenCV. Click one to jump to it.
  • Search, for example “badger”, to light up matching animals in every clip, or ask the agent a question.
